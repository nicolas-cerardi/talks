  • About 50 000 satellite trajectories to simulate...
  • Instead, we can model the density of satellite in the sky with an analytical model from [Bassa, Hainaut & Galadi-Enriquez, 2022]
  • We adapted this model and applied it to the SKAO in [Cerardi, Tolley, di Vruno, 2026]
  • So far, a geometrical prediction only (no forecast of the received power)

Forecasting the satellite occupancy in SKAO observations

  • At its latitude, SKA-Low is covered by most orbital shells






Code release

Analytical-Satsky
  • Public repo: github.com/nicolas-cerardi/Analytical-Satsky
  • v1: simple model as in [Bassa et al. 2022, Cerardi et al. 2026]. Allows fast reproducibility of the paper results
  • v2 (in progress): dropping simplifications for large FoVs, and fake satellite sampling
  • Users and feedback welcome !

Extending the model towards RFI forecasts

  • Requires an integral model, dropping small FoV assumptions from [Bassa et al. 2022]
  • Generate a catalogue of fake satellites with realistic trajectories
  • Assume transmitted power (e.g. from [di Vruno et al. 2023])
  • Interfaced with an interferometric simulator (e.g. Chris Finlay's tab-sim).

Take-aways

  • Satellites will transit in the SKA-Low beam for large parts of the observation time, up to 100% around 100 MHz and below, see the A&A Letter [Cerardi et al. 2026].
  • The code is fast, public and reusable for other telescopes and observations.
  • Currently extending the model towards actual RFI level forecasts.
