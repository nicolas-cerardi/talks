Analytical-Satsky
  • Public repo: github.com/nicolas-cerardi/Analytical-Satsky
  • v1: simple model as in [Bassa et al. 2022, Cerardi et al. 2026]. Allows fast reproducibility of the paper results
  • v2 (in progress): dropping simplifications for large FoVs, and fake satellite sampling
  • Users and feedback welcome !
